Behind the work

Curious about people, and about what makes them act.

  • Research firstI ask before I assume: 135 readers surveyed before writing a single recommendation.
  • Segment before you speakOne message per audience: six profiles at Cryptobel, each with its own tone and format, not one post for everyone.
  • Ship in every formatOne idea, nine formats: I like the craft of adapting a campaign without losing its identity.
  • Measure what happens nextGA4, GTM and Power BI let me follow a campaign after it ships, not just launch it.

I studied Marketing at EPHEC in the Arts & Culture track, which is where the manga publishing projects come from: audiences I know well, and a market where a small publisher has to be inventive to be seen.

Since then I have segmented about 8,000 customer profiles, built Power BI dashboards on marketing KPIs, written press releases, run social media accounts and built a clothing brand’s content. The Business Data Analysis specialisation came after, so that what a marketing team creates can be measured and improved.
