01 · Context

A young clothing brand that needed a consistent voice on social media.

Akira Fashion is a clothing brand run as a real entrepreneurial project. Like any young brand, it had to become visible and desirable with limited means: a recognisable universe, regular content and clear communication around collections and commercial moments.

I contributed on the communication and product side, from the content published on social media to the design of accessories and the way products were presented.

02 · Challenge

Make every piece of content look like the same brand.

How do you present a collection, announce a promotion and highlight a product so that each post strengthens the same brand identity?

The answer was a small set of visual codes applied to every format, and content planned around the brand's key moments.

05 · Brand universe

Few codes, applied everywhere.

What the published content shows about the brand's identity.

Palette

Black and white, almost nothing else.

Promotional and product visuals share the same monochrome base, so a sale story and a collection story read as one brand.

Typography

Large, bold, uppercase.

Headlines carry the message on their own; the copy stays short (“Fall / Winter collection”, “Save up to 25%”).

Logo

The A-mark and the wordmark, always present.

Placed as a signature on every visual, small on promotions, large on lifestyle imagery.

Product

Statement pieces with a Japanese reference.

The “Wise-Man” sweatshirt pairs an English word with its Japanese characters, in line with the brand name.

Motifs

Repeated “SALE” tape for promotions.

A single graphic device that signals a commercial moment without leaving the monochrome universe.

Imagery

Lifestyle photography for collections.

Products are shown worn, in a real setting, rather than on a flat background.

09 · Key learnings
“A brand is what stays the same from one post to the next. Consistency does more for recognition than any single visual.”
  • Less copy, stronger visualOn a story, the headline and the picture do the work. Everything else is noise.
  • Commercial moments need their own codeThe SALE motif makes a promotion recognisable without leaving the brand universe.
  • Show only what you can back upWithout documented figures, the honest portfolio shows the work and stops there.
