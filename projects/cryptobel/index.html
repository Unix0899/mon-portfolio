01 · Business problem

Cryptobel had an audience, but little structured data about who it was.

Cryptobel helps a French-speaking audience understand Web3 and cryptocurrencies. The organisation had little structured information to identify precisely who its audience was, what it was looking for and how it expressed itself.

How can Cryptobel better identify and segment its French-speaking audience to improve its communication strategy?

04 · Data collection

Two public sources, one database.

Reddit

Python + PRAW

Posts from r/CryptoFR and r/BitcoinFrance containing a crypto keyword and at least 3 upvotes, with up to 10 comments each. Collected daily through the Windows Task Scheduler.

58 posts · 361 comments

Instagram

Apify + Python

Public posts and first comments from five French-speaking crypto accounts, collected three times a week and imported from CSV.

906 posts · 660 comments

07 · Clustering

Why K-means?

HDBSCAN was tested first, then set aside: it labelled many messages as noise and produced very small, unstable clusters. K-means gave more stable and more interpretable groups, and let us fix the number of segments.

k was tested from 2 to 10. The elbow curve flattens after k = 5, and the silhouette score reaches a local maximum at k = 5 (≈ 0.43). Higher values of k scored only marginally better, at the cost of more complexity and less stable groups, so k = 5 was kept for both platforms.

11 · Complementary study

22 interviews with people affected by crypto scams.

The project also looked at people living in Belgium who had contacted Cryptobel after a crypto-related scam. A structured questionnaire (Google Forms) was completed during phone interviews from 19 to 23 May 2025, with respondents aged 30 to 75.

22participants
30–75age range
~15 minaverage interview duration

13 of the 22 respondents described themselves as beginners, and 12 had been interested in crypto for less than six months. Fake investment websites were the most common scam (13 cases). A professional-looking website (13) and promises of high returns (9) were the main reasons people trusted the scam. Afterwards, most stopped investing (16 of 22). Half said yes to educational prevention content, and 4 more said maybe, with explanatory videos (12) and practical guides (10) as preferred formats.

A star-schema model normalised the multi-answer questionnaire data so it could be analysed flexibly in Power BI.

12 · Business insights

What the data showed.

Insight 01

Reddit audiences showed stronger demand for practical, transparent and risk-oriented information.

Insight 02

Instagram audiences were more emotionally engaged and receptive to community-driven communication.

Insight 03

Beginner and cautious audiences showed a strong need for educational content about risks and security.

13 · Recommendations

Different audiences should not receive the same message in the same format.

For cautious users

Tutorials, security guides and comparison content.

For enthusiastic audiences

Community content, stories, events and participative formats.

For sceptical audiences

Transparency, evidence and clear communication about risk.

For technical users

Advanced resources, webinars and technical collaboration.

For scam prevention

Warning-sign checklists, victim stories and educational videos.

Across channels

Keep the core message consistent, but adapt tone, depth and format to each platform.

14 · Limitations

What the analysis can and cannot claim.

  • Only French-speaking public communities were analysed.
  • Reddit and Instagram have different content structures, which creates a platform bias.
  • Social-media sentiment can be noisy.
  • Clusters describe discourse patterns, not fixed identities.
  • Results support decisions; they are not an absolute truth about customers.
