01 · Context & business problem

Every tool had its own numbers. Nobody saw the chain from a euro spent to a customer who stays.

ODRES Group develops an ERP (odres.io) that lets restaurants, shops and other businesses manage their establishment through several connected applications. Growth depends on acquiring these businesses as subscribers through several channels: paid ads, organic search, direct traffic, email, partners and referrals.

Spend lives in the ad platforms, sessions in web analytics, leads and deals in the CRM, customers and recurring revenue in billing. Channels are easily compared on lead volume instead of customers and revenue, and data issues silently distort totals.

Which acquisition channels deserve more investment, which ones create low-quality volume, and where does the funnel lose value?

05 · Data quality

The raw data is messy on purpose, and the pipeline has to prove it fixed it.

  • Duplicate rows
  • Inconsistent labels
  • Invalid dates
  • Negative spend
  • Outliers
  • SQL without MQL
  • Unknown campaigns
  • Orphan deals

The exports contain 20 kinds of controlled anomalies. The same 22 checks run before and after cleaning; the pipeline stops if a blocking issue remains.

06 · SQL analysis

The reporting layer is built in SQL, not assembled by hand.

A schema with keys and business rules as constraints, six reusable views and 38 analysis queries: funnel by channel and campaign, CPL and CAC, conversion at each stage, revenue share, deal value, sales cycle, month-over-month growth, MRR and churn.

CTEJOIN / LEFT JOINCASE WHENGROUP BY / HAVINGSubqueriesLAGRANK / DENSE_RANKNULLIF / COALESCE
-- Campaigns with high spend and weak conversion
SELECT campaign_name, channel_name, spend, leads,
       ROUND(100.0 * lead_to_sql_rate, 1) AS lead_to_sql_pct,
       customers, cac, recommendation
FROM vw_campaign_performance
WHERE spend > (SELECT AVG(spend) FROM vw_campaign_performance
               WHERE spend > 0)
  AND lead_to_sql_rate < (SELECT 1.0 * SUM(is_sql) / COUNT(*)
                          FROM fact_leads)
ORDER BY spend DESC;

10 · Key insights

What the synthetic dataset shows.

In the synthetic demonstration dataset — these are not ODRES results. What matters is the reasoning from a number to a decision.

Insight 01

Lead volume is misleading.

Meta Ads brings 28% of leads but only 2% of customers, with a CAC of €3,534 for an average deal of €892.

Insight 02

A third of the budget returns almost nothing.

Four campaigns spent €37.6k (33% of spend) for €6.7k of first-year revenue.

Insight 03

A higher CAC can be the better investment.

LinkedIn has the highest cost per lead, but the largest deals (€3,398) and a 40% win rate.

Insight 04

Non-paid channels carry growth.

Referral, email and organic: 18% of spend, 84% of first-year revenue.

Insight 05

The lead score works.

Leads scoring 80+ convert at 20.9%, those below 40 at 0.8%.

Insight 06

Company size drives value, not region.

Companies with 50+ staff: 10% of leads, 43% of revenue. Regions convert within a narrow 5.6–8.0% band.

11 · Recommendations

Every paid campaign gets a clear budget decision.

SCALE

Revenue ≥ 1.5× spend and above-average lead quality. Increase LinkedIn "Operations Leaders" step by step, watching CAC against deal value.

MAINTAIN

Pays back in about a year. Keep Google "High Intent Search" and optimise bids rather than volume.

OPTIMISE

Between 0.5× and 1×. Rework targeting and landing pages before adding budget.

REDUCE

Below 0.5×. Pause Meta "Awareness Video" and Google "Competitor Search" first; test with a 3-month hold-out.

Sales follow-up

Route leads scoring 60+ to sales within 24 hours; send leads below 40 to automated nurture.

Growth engine

Formalise a customer-referral programme and expand partners: lowest CAC, highest lead quality.

13 · Limitations

What this case study can and cannot claim.

  • The data is synthetic: patterns are realistic by design, not measured at ODRES.
  • Attribution is first-touch (the lead's source in the CRM); multi-touch journeys are not modelled.
  • Revenue is first-year contract value; lifetime value would favour channels with lower churn.
  • Some groups are small (7 Meta customers), so recommendations are framed as tests.
  • The screenshots show the report without filters; the interactive version is the Power BI project in the repository.
