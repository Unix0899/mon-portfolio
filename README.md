# Harry Mulembwe — Portfolio

Personal portfolio of Harry Mulembwe, Brussels. One hybrid profile, Marketing · Digital · Data, with two entry points by role (Data & Business Analyst, Digital Marketing Officer) and eight case studies.
Available in English, French and Dutch.

```text
index.html                    Landing page: animated welcome, how the portfolio works, the two profile doors, why both, contact
data/index.html               Profile 01 · Data & Business Analyst (hero, workflow, data case studies, personal project, skills, about, CV, contact)
marketing/index.html          Profile 02 · Marketing & Communication Officer (hero, marketing projects, skills, about, CV, contact)
styles.css                    Styles shared by the landing and the two profile pages
projects/cryptobel/           Cryptobel case study

assets/i18n/projects.js       FR + NL translations of the all-projects page
assets/i18n/cryptobel.js      FR + NL translations of the Cryptobel case study
```

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.

## Translations

English text lives directly in the HTML. Every translatable element has a
`data-i18n="key"` attribute; the French and Dutch versions of that key are in
`assets/i18n/<page>.js`. To change a sentence, edit it in the HTML (English) and
in the matching key of the translation file (FR + NL).

A language can be forced with a URL parameter, e.g. `/?lang=nl` or
`/projects/cryptobel/?lang=fr`. The visitor's choice is remembered across pages.

## Run locally

```bash
python -m http.server 3000
```

Then open http://localhost:3000.

Pushing to the `main` branch on GitHub triggers a new Vercel deployment.

## LexLau · Klavis (synthetic reconstruction)

`projects/lexlau/proof/` holds the 8 proofs generated by the public repository
`lexlau-klavis-ai-workflow-demo` (`scripts/make_proofs.py`: screenshots of the running demo, SQL and
bug cards rendered from the repository). Every figure on the page comes from that repository's
database, tests and SQL views. Synthetic demonstration data: no real client, legal-case or
confidential LexLau/Klavis data. Never present the project as "I built Klavis".

## Belgian EV Charging Infrastructure Intelligence (personal project, official open data)

`projects/ev-charging/` is a data case study presented with the punch of the marketing pages. It loads
`assets/marketing-case.css` (shared base) and its own `styles.css`, which holds the whole "electric" theme
(night ink `#0b1330` + volt `#c8ff32`, hero collage, ticker, offset-shadow cards). It does not depend on the
stylesheet of another case study. All motion is disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

On the data profile it is not in the list of professional case studies: it has its own "Personal project"
section (`#personal`, styles in the `.pp-*` block at the end of `styles.css`).

Every text and figure comes from the project's `PORTFOLIO_CASE_STUDY.md`, `INSIGHT_LOG.md`, `README.md` and
`POWERBI_SQL_RECONCILIATION.md` (55 DAX measures, 75 / 75 values equal to SQL).

| File in `proof/` | What it is |
|---|---|
| `powerbi_01_executive_overview.jpg`, `powerbi_02_geographic_coverage.jpg`, `powerbi_03_opportunity_areas.jpg` | Real, unedited screenshots of the three Power BI pages (Power BI Desktop). Used in the hero collage, the dashboard section, the data-profile showcase and the OG image |
| `fig_01` ... `fig_07_*.jpg` | Analysis figures generated by the Python pipeline (`python -m src.figures`), JPEG, max 1600 px. Never caption them as Power BI screenshots |

Rules kept on the page: gaps are relative, not measured shortages; the coverage index is project-created, not
official; household cars are a demand proxy, not EV counts; no figure that is not in the sources.

## Marketing & Communication (profile 02)

The site has two profile pages reached from the landing page: `01 · Data, Business & Analytics` (Cryptobel, ODRES, LexLau, EV Charging) and
`02 · Marketing & Communication` (Komogi, Mangetsu, Akira Fashion, LJE + Mini-Entreprises Sales Day).
The marketing case studies follow one structure: Context, Challenge, My role, Approach, Insights,
Strategy / Solution, Deliverables, Skills, Key learnings, Next project.

| Project | Type shown on the page | Source of the figures and images |
|---|---|---|
| Komogi | Individual project (TFE, EPHEC 2022–2023) | TFE defence deck: 135 respondents, 8 interviews, 87 %, 70.9 %, 61.5 %, 81.5 %, 12-month media planning, €1,643.60 excl. VAT |
| Mangetsu | Team project (5 students) | Team deck: 4-stage journey, 9 recommendations, prototypes, quantitative survey charts |
| Akira Fashion | Entrepreneurial project (Photoshop + Canva) | 7 web banners, 7 lifestyle mock-ups, 10 product visuals, 2 mobile banners, 2 stories (28 visuals). No sales, followers or engagement figures are published |
| LJE | Internship (Communication Intern, Feb – May 2023, Canva + Photoshop) | Press release "Boost Your Planet", Boost Your Talent press kit 2023 (5 pages), 9 formats of the Mini-Entreprises Sales Day campaign, 3 contest stories, coach recruitment poster, LJE Challenge 2023 award cheques (13 designs, 17 May 2023) |

Rules kept on every page: survey figures are never presented as commercial results, academic strategies are
labelled as recommendations, the Mangetsu page credits the team, and no KPI, date, tool or budget is invented.
Images in `projects/<slug>/proof/` are JPEG exports (max 1600 px, < 300 KB) of the original PDF / PNG deliverables.

The Komogi and Mangetsu case studies share a "manga edition" theme in `assets/manga-case.css` (panel layout, halftone, speech bubble, translated ticker, pop reveals). Each project only sets its palette in its own `styles.css`: yellow and black for Komogi, red and black for Mangetsu. All motion is disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

The Akira Fashion case study has its own dark streetwear theme in `projects/akira/styles.css` (glitch title, neon ticker, lookbook grid, banner rail).

The LJE case study has its own pop theme at the end of `projects/lje/styles.css` (colours of the LJE logo, one accent per chapter, confetti, rainbow ticker, bouncing reveals).
